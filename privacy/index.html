This policy explains what information the Floor Fit app uses and why. We wrote it to be short and clear.

1. Summary

  • Floor Fit has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your game progress, settings, hints, coins and streak are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device data as described below.
  • Floor Fit Pro is sold through Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves levels completed, stars, coins, hints left, daily streak, sound/music/haptic settings, album theme and whether Floor Fit Pro is active. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you have not subscribed to Floor Fit Pro, the app shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (Android)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itAdvertising, fraud prevention
Device and app information, ad interactions, diagnosticsAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention

Data handled by Google is covered by Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent: In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, the app shows Google's consent message (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad Privacy Choices (shown where it applies). You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

4. Purchases (Floor Fit Pro)

Floor Fit Pro is an optional auto-renewing weekly subscription processed by Google Play. Google handles payment and may share with us only what is needed to confirm the subscription (for example a purchase token and its status). We do not receive your card or bank details. You can cancel anytime in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Google Play's terms apply.

5. Children

Floor Fit is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested in a way that is suitable for a general audience. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. We do not share data with anyone other than Google (AdMob and Google Play) as described above.

7. Security and retention

Data on your device stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's policies. Connections to Google services use encryption in transit.

8. Your rights

Because we do not keep personal data on our own systems, there is nothing for us to access or delete. You control on-device data by clearing app data or uninstalling. For data processed by Google, use your Google account controls or contact Google. You may contact us with any privacy question at the email above.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
