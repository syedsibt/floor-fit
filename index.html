Floor Fit: Room Plan Puzzle

Fit every room into the house plan and connect all the doors. An offline floor plan puzzle with 1,500 levels and a new house every day.

Get it on Google Play

How to play

  1. Drag each room (Kitchen, Bedroom, Bath, Living and more) into the empty house outline.
  2. Rooms cannot overlap or stick out of the house.
  3. Every door must meet a door in the next room, and one door must meet the Main Entrance.
  4. In Medium and Hard, tap a room to turn it.

Features

  • 1,500 hand-checked levels: Easy, Medium and Hard (500 each)
  • Daily House: a new house every day, with a day streak
  • Blueprint Album: collect 75 finished house plans
  • Fully offline: no account, no sign-in, no server
  • Hints, undo and relaxing music
  • Optional Floor Fit Pro (weekly): no ads, unlimited hints, extra album themes

Questions

Does Floor Fit work without internet?

Yes. Every level is inside the app. Internet is only used for ads and for buying Pro through Google Play.

Is Floor Fit free?

Yes, it is free to play with ads. Floor Fit Pro is an optional weekly subscription that removes ads. You can cancel it anytime in Google Play > Subscriptions.

Who is Floor Fit for?

Anyone who likes tangram, block and logic puzzles, home design, or a calm brain game for short breaks.
